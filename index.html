Courage the Cowardly Dog

Courage the Cowardly Dog is a surreal American animated horror comedy series that follows an easily frightened pink canine named Courage, who lives in an isolated farmhouse in the middle of Nowhere, Kansas, with his elderly owners: the sweet-natured, caring Muriel Bagge and her grumpy, greedy husband Eustace. Despite his overwhelming paranoia and constant terror, Courage must repeatedly overcome his fears to heroically defend his oblivious family from an endless onslaught of paranormal threats, including bizarre monsters, aliens, demons, and mad scientists. Known for its dark humor, unsettling atmosphere, and experimental animation styles, the show ultimately centers on themes of love and loyalty, proving that true bravery means stepping up to protect those you care about despite being completely terrified.
